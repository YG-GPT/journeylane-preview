# Operator sources — how this directory is built

Updated 2026-08-21. (Earlier versions of this file described only the first
19 hand-checked listings; they are preserved in the workspace archive.)

## What is listed

- **166 live operators** (operators.json) across Ontario and the Outaouais.
- **The original 19** were verified by loading each operator's own website by
  hand (2026-08-15).
- **The rest were found through Google Places searches** (2026-08-19) filtered
  for businesses whose own name or Google classification says they rent, then
  cleaned on 2026-08-21: regions re-derived from each business's coordinates,
  categories corrected from business names, duplicates collapsed, and
  listings that were competitor-marketplace pages, social-media-only pages,
  wrong websites, or non-recreation businesses removed or held back.
- **A reserve of unpublished candidates** (marinas, tour operators, clubs)
  is held for human review and is not shown on the site.
- Item-level rentals and prices are extracted from operators' own public
  websites, dated, and shown with the note that rates are as published on the
  operator's site.

## Data policy

- P2P marketplace listings (Outdoorsy, RVezy, GetMyBoat and similar) are
  excluded: a listing must be the operator's own business, not an entry on
  another platform. (Three that slipped through an automated sweep were
  removed 2026-08-21.)
- Google-served content (photos, ratings, map data) is requested live from
  Google under Google Maps Platform terms, not permanently stored here.
- Any listed business can have its listing corrected or removed by emailing
  journeylaneinc@gmail.com (subject: "Remove my listing"). Requests are acted
  on.
