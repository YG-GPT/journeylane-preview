Privacy

This site is a work-in-progress preview of JourneyLane. It is not a booking service yet.

Last updated: 2026-08-21.

What this site collects

  • Nothing is transmitted to us automatically. The site has no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no account system.
  • Favourites you save are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and never leave your device.
  • The "Request to book" form is a demonstration: it sends nothing anywhere. Any details you type stay on your device and disappear when you close the page.
  • If you email us (contact or "remove my listing"), we receive what you send and use it only to answer you.

Third-party content

  • Business photos, star ratings, and map data on operator cards are served by Google under Google Maps Platform terms.
  • The map view is rendered with Leaflet/OpenStreetMap.
  • Fonts are served by Google Fonts.
  • These providers may receive standard technical data (such as your IP address) when your browser loads their content.

Business listings

Listings are compiled from publicly available information about local rental businesses. If your business is listed and you want it corrected or removed, email journeylaneinc@gmail.com and we will act on it.
