What JourneyLane is (today)

JourneyLane is a directory of recreation-rental businesses in Ontario and the Outaouais, compiled from publicly available information. JourneyLane is a venue for finding rental operators — it is not a party to any rental, does not process bookings or payments, and does not own, inspect, or operate any equipment listed.

About the information shown

  • Listing details (categories, items, prices) come from public sources including operators' own websites, and may be out of date. Always confirm details, availability, and prices directly with the operator.
  • JourneyLane makes no representations about any operator — including their licensing, insurance, safety practices, or equipment condition. Any such facts, where shown, are supplied by the operator or a public source, not asserted by JourneyLane.
  • Photos and star ratings on operator cards are provided by Google. Some item pictures are shown for illustration only.

Your rental is with the operator

Any rental you arrange is a contract between you and that business, on their terms. Requirements such as operator licences, age limits, safety equipment, and deposits are set by the operator and by law.
